Asset class · Energy

Energy.

Active positions across the physical and financial energy stack — crude, refined product, LNG, gas, geothermal, and structured derivative exposures. We participate as principal in markets where storage, blending, and offtake capability translate into structural advantage.

01 · OIL & GAS

Crude, refined, and LNG.

Trading the physical leg, not just the screen-traded one. Physical and paper positions across Brent, WTI, Dubai, and ICE benchmarks. Storage capacity and blending optionality let us extract value from grade differentials, term contracts, and seasonal carry that single-asset funds cannot reach.

LNG offtake structures and pipeline-gas exposure complement the crude/refined book — sized through structured derivatives and term contracts with credit-worthy counterparties.

Key activity
  • Crude (Brent · WTI · Dubai · ICE) — physical & paper
  • Refined products — diesel, gasoline, jet, naphtha
  • LNG offtake & pipeline gas
  • Storage capacity in Asian & Atlantic basins
Physical participation

Capacity where it matters.

Physical storage and blending capacity — Atlantic and Asian basins — backed by long-term tank leases and dedicated blending capability.

This is what separates a real principal energy book from a paper trading desk. We own and control the physical handling capacity that enables the trades, not just the screens that show them.

02 · GEOTHERMAL

Closed-Loop Geothermal.

Selective positions in next-generation deep geothermal. Closed-loop "advanced geothermal" technologies — OKAN CEEG (Geo-Plutonic), Eavor, Fervo — enable baseload renewable power generation from any sedimentary or crystalline basement, at scales relevant to data-center and grid-stabilization markets.

We participate selectively, sized against energy-transition macro framework, and frequently in structures hedged with commodity (BKGT) positions for portfolio-level risk balance.

  • Adjacent technologies — Fervo, GA Drilling, QHeat

03 · CARBON, PGM & HYDROGEN

The transition adjacency.

Strategic positions where energy markets meet metals. Selective participation in carbon-credit markets (EU ETS, voluntary, Article 6 sovereign credits), platinum group metals at the intersection of automotive catalysis and the hydrogen economy, and structured positions in hydrogen-PGM supply chains.

This adjacency is exactly where our mining-asset book and our energy book reinforce each other — a feature few multi-asset platforms can replicate without rebuilding internally.

Key activity
  • EU ETS & voluntary carbon market positions
  • Article 6 sovereign carbon credit instruments
  • PGM-hydrogen catalyst supply-chain positioning
  • Hydrogen project minority equity (selective)
Energy market view

The structural setup we see.

A decade of upstream underinvestment colliding with energy-transition demand. Conventional barrels remain essential; LNG demand grows in Asia; gas substitutes coal; renewables scale fast but require base-load complement.

  • Oil — structural under-investment + OPEC discipline = supportive base case
  • Gas / LNG — Asian demand growth, European supply rebalancing, US export expansion
  • Geothermal — closed-loop technologies open base-load renewable economics
  • Carbon — Article 6 sovereign credits emerging as institutional asset class
  • PGM & hydrogen — convergent demand from EV catalysis and H2 catalysis
