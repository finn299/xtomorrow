Technology

Technology that makes the assets better.

Two layers — the physical-asset layer (PCE / EDMSU mining technology) and the issuance layer (institutional-grade blockchain infrastructure). Both operated through wholly-owned or majority-controlled subsidiaries.

01

PCE Mining Technology

Potential-Controlled Electrolysis — cyanide-free, low-temperature, selective metal recovery. 90% gold yield from refractory feed. Validated at KGHM (2013), ENAMI (2020), Elemental Poland (2025). US Patent App. 20150197867.

EDMSU

Electrodynamic Magnetic Separation Unit — dry, water-free metal enrichment for arid environments and refractory ores. 99.0–99.2% recovery at fine particle sizes (50-100 µm). Mobile, modular, containerized.

Blockchain Infrastructure
