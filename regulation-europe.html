Europe

MiCA + eWpG + DLT Pilot.

The most comprehensive cross-border digital-asset regulatory framework. MiCA, eWpG (Germany), and the DLT Pilot Regime together enable institutional tokenized issuance with full legal recognition and EU passporting.

01

MiCA (EU-wide)

First comprehensive cross-border regime for crypto-assets in a major jurisdiction. Authorisation, prudential, conduct-of-business, and disclosure standards for asset-referenced and e-money tokens.

02

eWpG (Germany)

Electronic Securities Act grants full legal recognition to electronic securities recorded on a crypto-securities register. Bonds, shares, and fund units can be issued natively on-chain with BaFin oversight.

03

DLT Pilot Regime

Permits operation of multilateral trading facilities and settlement systems using DLT, with explicit derogations from MiFID II and CSDR to enable institutional pilots.

04

Liechtenstein TVTG

One of the world's most progressive token frameworks. Clean classification, fast setup, EEA passporting via Liechtenstein membership.

05

Luxembourg

Established fund domicile with explicit DLT support. Compatible with UCITS and AIFMD structures — ideal for tokenized fund wrappers distributable EU-wide.

06

France & Malta

PACTE Act (France) and VFA Act (Malta) provide additional issuance pathways with bespoke licensing for crypto and tokenized-securities issuers.
