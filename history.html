History

Three decades of converting technology into industries.

XTOMORROW is the fourth platform built by the firm's founding leadership — following telecommunications, IoT, and the commercial space industry. Each was, in its time, considered too early. Each is now mainstream.

01

1997 — 2001 · Telecommunications

Co-founded pioneering a transformational frontier-technology platform. Successfully merged with a NASDAQ-listed company (SONS) in 2001 — the technology transformed the global telecom cost base.

02

2004 · Aerospace (global incentive program)

Title sponsorship of the a landmark global incentive program — the first incentive prize for private human spaceflight. Won by SpaceShipOne; widely credited as the catalyst for the modern commercial space industry (the modern commercial space industry).

03

2006 — present · IoT & Smart Infrastructure

Co-founded an IoT services platform, a leading IoT services company. Its technology underpins media streaming devices, smart-home solutions, smart speakers, elder-care systems, and connected cars worldwide.

04

2024 — present · Real-World Asset Tokenization

Launched XTOMORROW as a multi-asset investment platform with a native STO and RWA tokenization engine. Anchored by the 60-year YMAD PPP (Argentina), the BKGT Sharia-compliant gold-token program (Singapore), and the PCE / EDMSU technology stack.
