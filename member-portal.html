Welcome to the XTOMORROW data room.

A private portal for verified institutional investors, family offices, sovereign vehicles, operating partners, and authorised counterparties. Access is invitation-only and protected by two-factor verification.

How portal access works

A simple three-step verification.

Every login is gated by three independent layers. Each layer is enforced separately, audit-logged, and timestamped. You cannot bypass one with another.

01 / Email verification

Confirm your identity

When you visit the portal URL, Cloudflare Access sends a one-time 6-digit PIN to your registered email. You enter the PIN to prove you control the address. The session is bound to that device.

02 / Access code

Enter your portal code

After email verification, you enter an alphanumeric access code issued by your XTOMORROW relationship partner. The code determines which workstreams, projects, and document trees you can view.

03 / Confidentiality

Accept the NDA terms

On first entry and on every materially-updated session, you acknowledge the confidentiality conditions covering the materials. The acknowledgement is stored as a timestamped, IP-bound record.

Security & privacy

How we protect your access.

Portal security is enforced at four independent layers, each industry-standard and audit-logged. No single breach exposes the underlying materials.

Layer 1
Cloudflare Access
Email-verified PIN authentication. Every login attempt is logged with timestamp, IP geolocation, and device fingerprint.
Layer 2
Access Code
Portal-specific alphanumeric code issued by your XTOMORROW relationship partner. Determines scope of document visibility.
Layer 3
Confidentiality & Watermarking
Per-session NDA acknowledgement. Documents are dynamically watermarked with reviewer identity and timestamp. Downloads restricted by tier.
Layer 4
Restricted Drive
Underlying files reside on enterprise Google Drive shared only with the portal service account. No public Drive link is ever generated.
XTOMORROW will never email you a password.
Access is verified by a one-time PIN sent to your registered email at the start of each session.
