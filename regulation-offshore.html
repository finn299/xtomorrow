Offshore

Speed and structure for global sophisticated capital.

Offshore jurisdictions complement onshore issuance with speed-of-setup, tax efficiency, and flexibility — particularly for fund-of-tokens vehicles, Index Token SPVs, and multi-asset wrappers distributed to international institutional capital.

Cayman Islands

Offshore-friendly, no specific crypto taxes, and flexible SPV structures commonly used for tokenized funds and securities. Well-established for private placements with international institutional appeal.

British Virgin Islands

Similar to Cayman — flexible corporate law, no capital-gains tax, and a supportive environment for global sophisticated-investor security-token structures.

Gibraltar

One of the earliest DLT regulatory frameworks. Proportionate licensing, flexible structure, low barriers to entry. Strong fit for institutional-only STO programmes.

Liechtenstein

TVTG Blockchain Act provides one of the most progressive token-classification regimes globally, paired with EEA passporting via Liechtenstein-EEA membership.
