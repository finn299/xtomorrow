Asset Classes

Seven markets. One integrated portfolio.

We operate across asset classes that traditional firms separate into discrete fund vehicles. Each is run by specialists with deep sector experience, integrated under a single capital allocation framework.

01 · Energy & Commodities

Energy & Commodities

Active positions across the physical and financial energy stack. We trade oil, refined products, and natural gas across global benchmarks. Positions span physical cargo flows, term contracts, and derivative exposures held against fundamental supply-demand views.

Storage capacity, blending capabilities, and selective midstream interests provide structural advantages — we participate in the physical market, not merely the screen-traded one.

Activity profile
  • Crude and refined product trading across Atlantic and Asian basins
  • Natural gas and LNG term positions
  • Storage capacity and blending optionality
  • Selective midstream infrastructure interests
02 · Mining & Critical Minerals

Mining & Critical Minerals

Direct concessions, royalty interests, and proprietary processing technology. Precious metals (gold, silver), platinum group metals, copper, lithium, and rare-earth elements. We participate as direct operator under long-duration concessions, as royalty/streaming counterparty, and as licensor of next-generation processing technology (PCE / EDMSU).

Underwritten with operator-grade discipline: NI 43-101 / S-K 1300 verification, PCAOB-audited financials, environmental baseline, and offtake structuring — never narrative.

Activity profile
  • Argentina YMAD 60-year PPP: 54,000 ha Peirano Block
  • Nevada Newcastle Claims: $317.5B NI 43-101 in-situ value
  • PCE / EDMSU technology licensing (KGHM, ENAMI, Elemental validated)
  • Royalty & streaming positions in producing operations
03 · Real Estate

Real Estate

Direct ownership and structured equity in income-producing real assets. Focused on properties with durable cash flow, strong sponsor alignment, and pricing dislocation. We participate as principal across logistics, residential, hospitality, and selective commercial assets — increasingly accessed via tokenized vehicles for institutional liquidity.

Joint ventures with established regional operators give us local expertise without rebuilding it internally. Capital structure flexibility lets us provide equity, preferred equity, or hybrid capital depending on the situation.

Activity profile
  • Logistics and industrial assets in primary corridors
  • Multi-family residential with operating-partner JVs
  • Hospitality (selective, brand-affiliated)
  • Preferred equity and structured credit on real assets
  • Sharia-compliant Ijara / Musharaka tranches
04 · Public Equities

Public Equities

Concentrated long-only and event-driven equity strategies. 10-15 high-conviction long-only positions across cyclicals, commodities, technology, and financial leverage themes. Event-driven sleeves capture M&A, spin-offs, refinancings, and capital-structure events with defined catalysts and limited downside.

Equity book integrated with the firm's macro and sector views — and overlaid with proprietary digital-asset positions where correlations diverge from historic patterns.

Activity profile
  • Concentrated long-only — high-conviction operating businesses
  • Event-driven — M&A, spin-offs, capital-structure events
  • Mining & energy sector equities sized against direct positions
  • Macro overlays at the index, factor, and country level
05 · Fixed Income

Fixed Income

Sovereign, investment-grade credit, and tokenized debt participations. Treasuries, Bunds, JGBs, Gilts, and selective emerging-market sovereigns. Investment-grade credit positions and hand-picked high-yield with defined catalysts. Tokenized bond programmes (Germany eWpG, Singapore MAS, HK SFC) extend the conventional book onto on-chain rails.

Sukuk and Islamic-finance fixed-income participations open access to the USD 6T+ GCC and Southeast Asian Sharia-compliant institutional pool.

Activity profile
  • Sovereign and investment-grade credit
  • Tokenized bonds (eWpG / MAS / HK SFC)
  • Sukuk & Islamic fixed income
  • Stablecoin cash equivalents for operational treasury
06 · Digital Assets

Digital Assets

Core blockchain holdings, RWA tokens, and TradFi-Crypto hybrid positions. Strategic positions in Bitcoin and Ethereum sized as monetary-adjacent reserve assets. Curated Layer-2 and infrastructure exposure powering institutional RWA tokenization (Lumia, Polygon, Base, Avail). Proprietary security tokens issued by sister companies.

Tokenized US treasuries (BUIDL, BENJI, OUSG, USDY) deployed for on-chain cash management. TradFi-Crypto hybrids informed by the Crypto.com / Marszalek framework.

Activity profile
  • BTC & ETH — core monetary-reserve positions
  • Layer-2 & infrastructure (Lumia, Polygon, Base)
  • Proprietary security tokens (XT-GOLD-FN, BKGT, Index Token)
  • Tokenized treasuries & regulated stablecoins
07 · Tokenized Funds

Tokenized Funds

Wrapped multi-asset portfolios issued as Sharia-compliant security tokens. XTOMORROW's flagship issuance program — multi-asset baskets distributable globally via a single on-chain wrapper. Each fund is structured for institutional, accredited, and family-office investors across US, EU, UK, Asia, and the GCC, with Sharia and Halal sub-tranches as standard.

Issued under Reg D 506(c) + Reg S + MiCA + MAS + ADGM frameworks, custodied through Anchorage Digital and HSBC Metals Vault, and proof-verified through Chainlink PoR on every block.

Activity profile
  • Multi-Asset Index Token (global allocation)
  • Mining Token (Au · Ag · PGM · Cu pool)
  • Real-Asset Token (RE + infrastructure)
  • Sharia Index Token (AAOIFI-aligned wrapped multi-asset)
  • Digital Token (curated blockchain & RWA pool)
